## 1. PLANNING
* **In the owner's words (D51, D52, D53):** "build everything all of it should be free"; "I want to scrape everything possible"; feeds and articles, "public pages only: no logins, paywalls or anti-bot walls", news goes "into the AI evidence pack" and is logged as a model challenger that moves picks only after a walk-forward win. * **What it builds / replaces / is not:** builds a backend module that, on demand, reads two news feeds the owner approved the risk for (ESPN and CBS), cleans each headline, ties it to the teams and star players the app already knows, stores it once, and records how each source did.
