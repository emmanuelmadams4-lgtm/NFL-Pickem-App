# UX review of the spec, before any build (step 15 web)

Fresh read-only subagent. It read the first draft of section 2 (criteria L1 to L13), the 15d contract, the card, the game header, `design.md` and a 393 px screenshot. Nothing was run. 20 findings: 3 critical, 12 major, 5 minor. All decided below; the spec was rewritten as one state table (section 2 of the step log) before the first test.

| # | Sev | Finding | Decision (coordinator) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CRITICAL | A request failing from the start was specified twice, differently (L5 against L12), and "as today" means a kickoff time on a game under way. | Accepted. No live row ever shown: label "Started", "No live score right now", no third line, no "Not updating" word. |
| 2 | CRITICAL | A good reply with no row for a started game was not covered: first minute after kickoff, after midnight Eastern when an ended row leaves the reply, a game stuck over 8 hours. | Accepted. No row and none seen: "Started". No row, but an ended row was seen in this session: keep "Ended · result pending" with that score until the week data says final. |
| 3 | CRITICAL | Nothing started polling on a board left open across kickoff, and nothing moved "Ended" to "Final". | Accepted, smaller than proposed. Start rule: status `in_progress`, or the kickoff has passed on the clock `useLocked` uses and the game is not final or postponed. The week and game data already refetch every 120 s, which is what turns "Ended" into "Final"; no new refetch rule. Polling is 30 s while a started game is not ended, 120 s when every started game on screen is ended (so a reversal is still seen), none when nothing is started. No 10-minute timer. |
| 4 | MAJOR | Pregame numbers ("Model pick KC 87%", "Market KC 58%") read as current beside live ones. | Accepted: while a game is started they read "Pregame model pick" and "Pregame line"; one caption on the game screen. Keyed on the game's status, not on the live reply. |
| 5 | MAJOR | The stale rule flapped (one failed poll, return from a hidden page) and its placement was undefined. | Accepted: not updating when the reply says so or the last good reply is over 120 s old; one failed poll alone changes nothing; win chances are hidden then; the age comes from `last_confirmed_at`; `updated_at` is never used for staleness (it stands still through halftime), which is the contract's rule and departs from the plan row's wording on purpose. The reply's flag never marks an ended game. Not taken: a separate word "Offline" (the app's offline banner already says it). |
| 6 | MAJOR | "Stale", "confirmed", "Unconfirmed" are developer words. | Accepted: one word, "Not updating". |
| 7 | MAJOR | `lost` cannot show "the last numbers": both chances are null by contract. | Accepted: `lost` shows the score and "Not updating · last seen Q3 4:12", no chance line. Precedence written into the table. |
| 8 | MAJOR | "Now · ESPN KC 71% · Kalshi KC 68%" has no noun, repeats the club, reads as the bar's caption. | Accepted: "KC to win now · ESPN 71% · Kalshi 68%", after the uncertainty row; both missing: "No live win chance right now". |
| 9 | MAJOR | "Never 0% or 50%" would be tested as "no 0% ever"; rounding gives "100%" in a live game. | Accepted: a null is never drawn as a number; real values are whole percents, "<1%" and ">99%" at the ends; the away figure is 100 minus the rounded home figure. |
| 10 | MAJOR | Missing needs its reason (`design.md`). | Accepted on the game screen ("Kalshi – · no fresh price", "ESPN – · none right now"); the card keeps the short "Kalshi –". |
| 11 | MAJOR | The spec did not decide whether to hold the last non-null chance. | Decided: no hold. A null shows a dash at once; a value that comes back shows again. Holding would show a number the server no longer vouches for. Known cost: a dash can flicker for one poll. |
| 12 | MAJOR | Halftime reads "Q2 0:00" for 13 minutes; a delay cannot be seen. | Accepted: "Halftime", "End of Q1", "End of Q3", "End of Q4", "OT", "2OT"; any other clock is shown as it arrives. The game screen says "Checked 20s ago". A weather delay cannot be shown from period and clock alone: carried to god as a server item (pass ESPN's status text). |
| 13 | MAJOR | What gives way on the card was not named; the fixture was not the worst case. | Accepted: the "In progress" chip goes when the live label shows; the hit-rate sentence goes while a game is started; the phone-fit test uses three-letter clubs, two-digit scores, an upset watch, two QB alerts and a change note. |
| 14 | MAJOR | Two score sources with no precedence: an old week score could show as current. | Accepted: while started and not final the score comes only from the live reply; the week's scores are drawn only when the game is final. |
| 15 | MAJOR | One example sentence for screen readers does not cover the states. | Accepted: one sentence per state, in the table; visible parts hidden from screen readers; no live region. |
| 16 | MINOR | "Ended · awaiting final". | Accepted: "Ended · result pending"; no winner styling and no mark until the week data says final. |
| 17 | MINOR | Which club the live chance is stated for. | Accepted: the model's pick, then my pick, then the home club. One sentence on the game screen says what a Kalshi price is. |
| 18 | MINOR | Position on the game screen. | Accepted: label above the score in the centre column; the chip goes; the kickoff line stays. |
| 19 | MINOR | One score null, the other present. | Accepted: no score at all. |
| 20 | MINOR | Numerals shift width every poll. | Accepted: tabular numerals (existing utility, no new token). |
